01 / Built for Alberta

Quality Asphalt Shingle Systems, Specified For Alberta

Alberta doesn't test a roof gently. Between summer hail, chinook wind cycles and a freeze–thaw swing that can run 40 degrees in a week, most roof failures here start at the details — not the shingle. So that's where we spend our time.

  • Proven shingle systems We standardise on shingle systems our crews know inside out — IKO is our usual choice — and specify something different when a roof genuinely calls for it.
  • Ice & water shield where it counts Eaves, valleys, penetrations and low-slope transitions — the places Alberta ice damming actually attacks.
  • Balanced attic ventilation Intake and exhaust sized to your roof as part of the install, not guessed at. Poor ventilation shortens shingle life faster than storms do.

02 / Storm & Emergency

When The Weather Wins, We Move Fast

Wind lifts shingles, hail bruises them, and a roof that's been opened up doesn't wait politely. Get in touch as soon as you spot damage and we'll get the roof stabilised before the next front rolls through.

  • Temporary weatherproofing first Tarping and water control to stop the damage spreading into your ceilings, insulation and drywall.
  • A documented damage assessment Full roof, flashing and vent inspection with a written photo report, so you know exactly what failed and why.
  • Repair or replace, explained honestly Sometimes a section repair is the right answer. We'll tell you when that's the case rather than selling you a full roof.
  • One point of contact throughout The same person handles your job from first inspection to final walk-through.

07 / Roof Mapping

Know Exactly What Your Roof Needs — Before We Start

Before anyone quotes you a number, we build a precise digital map of your roof from satellite and aerial imagery. Every facet, pitch, edge, valley and ridge gets measured properly — and nobody has to climb up on your house to do it.

  • Measured, not eyeballed Roof area, pitch and every edge length come off the imagery to the inch, so the numbers your quote is built on are real measurements rather than someone's estimate from the driveway.
  • Exact material quantities Because the measurements are exact, we can calculate precisely how much material your roof takes — shingles, starter, ice and water shield, underlay, capping and flashing — at a sensible waste allowance.
  • A quote you can check You get the same report we price from. No padded estimates, no guesswork, no surprises once the job starts — you can see exactly what you're paying for and why.

10 / Roofing FAQs

Answers Before You Request an Estimate

The practical details homeowners most often want to confirm before getting started.

Where does SureSky provide roofing services?

SureSky Homes serves Edmonton first, along with Calgary, Sherwood Park, Leduc, Beaumont, Stony Plain, Spruce Grove and Airdrie.

Does SureSky provide free roofing estimates?

Yes. Every job starts with a free written estimate. SureSky also provides free roof mapping so the estimate is based on measured material quantities.

Is SureSky licensed and insured?

Yes. SureSky is a licensed roofing contractor carrying Commercial General Liability insurance. Certificates are available on request before work begins.

What roofing materials does SureSky Homes install?

IKO asphalt shingles are our standard on most jobs, matched to Alberta's wind, hail and freeze-thaw conditions. Where a roof calls for a different material, we will specify and install what suits it.

What roofing warranties are available?

SureSky offers three in-house warranty packages: Essential with a 10-year limited labour warranty, Plus with 15-year coverage, and Premium with 25-year coverage. Full terms are supplied in writing with every quote.
