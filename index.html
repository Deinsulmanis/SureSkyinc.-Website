02 / About SureSky Inc.

A Trade Business Run Like A Business

SureSky Inc. was founded in 2025, but the hands on your roof carry fifteen years of Alberta roofing behind them. The company exists to fix the part of this trade homeowners find hardest: knowing whether the quote in front of them is a fair one.

Most people getting a roof replaced do it once or twice in their life. They collect three quotes with three different scopes, three different warranties and three different sales pitches, then pick more or less at random. We built SureSky to be the one source that lays those options out plainly and explains what actually differs — then stands behind the work with a warranty we honour ourselves.

  • Alberta owned and operated Founded in 2025, based in Edmonton, working across the Edmonton and Calgary regions.
  • Fifteen years of hands-on roofing The experience behind the company predates the company — this isn't a first season on the roof.
  • Warranties honoured in-house We take full accountability for our own work. You call us, not an administrator.
  • Residential focus Homeowners are the whole business. We don't split our crews across commercial contracts.

03 / Our Standards

What Every Job Commits To

Whichever part of SureSky you deal with, these don't change.

Fully Insured, Every Job

Commercial General Liability insurance and WCB Alberta coverage on every job. Certificates supplied on request before work begins.

Licensed Contractor

SureSky is a licensed, registered roofing contractor operating in Alberta — not a seasonal crew passing through after a storm.

In-House Warranties

Three warranty packages, all written and honoured by SureSky. No third-party administrators, no fine print you find out about later.

Fixed Written Quotes

Line-item scopes with real numbers. If conditions change once the roof is opened up, you get a call and a price before anything proceeds.

Named Project Contact

One person responsible for your job from quote to sign-off. You always know who to call and you're never passed around.

Site Left Clean

Landscaping protected, debris removed, magnet sweep on every roofing job. We finish the property, not just the work.
