SureSky Inc. is an Alberta-owned company built around one simple idea: home services should feel different. We bring together skilled people, specialized trades and trusted services to create a better experience for homeowners. Starting with roofing through SureSky Homes, we are building toward the construction of homes in the community. We believe in doing things with intention, communicating honestly and creating work we are proud to put our name behind.

SureSky is not just about the work we do — it's about the trust we build along the way.

Who We Are

More Than a Home Services Company.

We're building a better way to care for your home.

Home improvement shouldn't feel complicated. Homeowners shouldn't have to become project managers, coordinate multiple contractors, wonder who to trust, or feel lost once the work begins.

SureSky Inc. was created to make that experience simpler. We are building a company around specialization, transparency, accountability and the homeowner experience.

Our divisions focus on their individual crafts, while SureSky Inc. provides the structure and standards that bring everything together.

One company. One standard.
